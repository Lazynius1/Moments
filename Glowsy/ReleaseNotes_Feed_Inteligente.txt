Novedades de Moments

- Los anillos de historias ahora diferencian mejor audiencias como Best Friends y Mutuals.

- Correcciones visuales del estado visto/no visto.

GUARDADOS RENOVADO
- Rediseño completo de la pantalla de guardados.
- Vista de detalle mas limpia y moderna.
- Mejor adaptacion a modo claro y oscuro.

COMPARTIR MOMENTOS
- El flujo de compartir se unifico en toda la app.
- Se elimino el duplicado de "copiar enlace".

ENLACES WEB DE MOMENTS
- Nueva pagina para abrir momentos compartidos por ID.

LISTAS Y AUDIENCIA
- Mejoras en gestion de listas personalizadas.
- Carga de miembros mas fiable.
- Mejor comportamiento cuando hay listas grandes.

PRIVACIDAD Y AJUSTES

- Ajustes de localizacion y textos en settings.
- Puedes eliminar momentos de tu colección si pasan a ser privados o restringidos.
- Hemos limpiado la interfaz: los momentos con restricciones ahora son más discretos y elegantes.
- Se ha reforzado la seguridad al compartir momentos que tienen restricciones de visualización.

En resumen:
Moments ahora es mas rapido, mas claro y mas comodo de usar en el dia a dia.

=== ENGLISH ===

Moments What's New

We've improved Moments to make everything feel faster, clearer, and more consistent.

Highlights of this version:

YOUR FRIENDS FIRST
- You now see the people you interact with most first.
- Best Friends are prioritized so you never miss anything important.
- This personalization is calculated on your device (we don't upload it to servers).
- If you reinstall the app, this affinity resets.
- Feed and stories now load much faster with lower battery consumption.

IMPROVED CHAT
- Videos load better and fail less.
- Video thumbnails are now generated when sending them.
- More consistent message statuses (sent, delivered, read).
- New in-chat search with navigation between results.
- New message separator and more useful "back to bottom" button.

CLEARER STORIES
- Story rings now better differentiate audiences like Best Friends and Mutuals.
- Your own ring also reflects which audience you posted for.
- Improvements in story activity view (audience, members, and lists).
- Visual fixes for seen/unseen status.

RENEWED SAVED CONTENT
- Complete redesign of the saved screen.
- Cleaner and more modern detail view.
- Better adaptation to light and dark modes.

MOMENT SHARING
- Sharing flow unified across the entire app.
- Duplicate "copy link" removed.
- Better integration with iOS native sharing.

MOMENTS WEB LINKS
- New page to open shared moments by ID.
- Better opening from deep links and universal links.
- New welcome popup with author's avatar and button to open Moments.
- Better loading of moment image and author profile photo.

LISTS AND AUDIENCE
- Improvements in custom list management.
- More reliable member loading.
- Better behavior with large lists.

PRIVACY AND SETTINGS
- Fixed content visibility based on privacy.
- Localization and text adjustments in settings.
- You can now remove moments from your collection if they become private or restricted.
- We've cleaned up the interface: restricted moments are now more discreet and elegant.
- Security has been reinforced when sharing moments with viewing restrictions.

In summary:
Moments is now faster, clearer, and more comfortable for everyday use.
