# Moments: The Future of Contextual Social Connection

"Moments" (com.glowsyapp) is a high-performance, privacy-first social networking platform built for the next generation of mobile interaction.

---

### 1. Design Excellence: The Liquid Glass System
Moments is built on the **Liquid Glass** aesthetic, moving beyond static interfaces to a UI that feels "alive" and premium.
- **Native Immersive Materials**: Extensive use of `ultraThinMaterial` and dynamic blur effects to create depth and focus.
- **Micro-Interactions & Haptics**: Every action, from a reaction to a "Story Chain" continuation, is backed by custom haptic feedback and particle animations.
- **Adaptive UI**: A sophisticated system of adaptive colors and gradients that respond to light/dark modes and content types, ensuring a consistent premium feel (curated palettes, zero "Teal").

- **Reactions with Intention**: We replaced the traditional "Like" with a diverse reaction system to reduce social pressure and foster authenticity. Features a usage-ranked picker and haptic particles for meaningful feedback.
- **Audience Segmentation & Lists**: Users manage visibility via 8 curated color palettes and icon sets. Granular control includes "Admirals", "Best Friends", and fully "Custom" manual lists.
- **Serial Collaboration (Story Chains)**: A rhythmic storytelling format with 48h windows and serial locking, ensuring shared experiences are captured in a structured, engaging way.
- **Precision Meta-Tagging**: A robust **normalized coordinate system** (0.0 – 1.0) ensures accurate user tagging across all device resolutions, integrated with a glassmorphic search interface.
- **Custom Drawing & Stickers**: Advanced PencilKit integration with Neon, Marker, and Arrow brushes for personal expression and collaborative creation.

### 3. Performance Architecture
Designed for low latency and high reliability, Moments manages data and media with unmatched efficiency.
- **Multi-Tier Video Caching**: 
  - **RAM-Level Preloader**: Buffers the next 6 high-priority assets based on user scrolling.
  - **Persistent Disk Cache**: SHA256-hashed local storage to enable instant, offline-first media playback.
- **Intelligent 2GB Storage Management**: An autonomous background service cleanses temporal/stale assets, maintaining a light device footprint without sacrificing speed.
- **Global Readiness**: Built-in native support for **Spanish, English, and Catalan**, with a highly modular localization engine.

### 5. Growth, Intent & Engagement
Moments uses deep behavioral analytics to iterate on user experience without compromising individual privacy.
- **Mirroring Engagement**: Nova AI adapts its communicative persona (emoji frequency, tone, length) to match the user's style, fostering genuine empathy.
- **Intentional Interaction**: Shifting from the "Passive Scroll" of legacy giants to "Active Connection" via Echoes and Story Chains.
- **Micro-Segmentation**: Engagement depth tracking through gesture patterns (swipes, long-press, haptic response latency).

### 6. Privacy, Security & Compliance
In an era of data mistrust, Moments takes a "Zero-Knowledge" inspired approach.
- **WhatsApp-Grade Encryption**: Robust `EncryptionService` managing user-specific rotating keys and conversation-bound AES-256 keys, backed by Apple's Keychain.
- **Resilient Recovery System**: Sophisticated device-change detection and master key validation to ensure users never lose access while maintaining top-tier security.
- **Granular Visibility**: Sophisticated logic layers (Mutual Blocks, Admiral-only views, Best Friend tiers) enforced at the Firestore security rule level.
- **Data Sovereignty**: Automated **Data Export Engine** (JSON/CSV) fulfilling GDPR/CCPA requirements for full data portability.
