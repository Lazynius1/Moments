# Moments

**A social app for iPhone and Android:** share posts, stories, and reels with per-post audiences, encrypted chat, map memories, Echoes, and less like-pressure.

Moments is a private social network where every moment stays yours. Share your day with the people you choose—without feeling like everything has to be perfect, public, or made to please everyone. Photos, videos, stories, messages, places, memories, and the little things that only make sense with your friends.

Built to feel closer: less noise, more intention, and more control over what you share.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/moments-haz-amigos-y-conecta/id6749241395) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.momentsapp) · [momentsapp.app](https://momentsapp.app/)

Moments isn’t about posting more. It’s about sharing better—with the people who truly matter.

## What makes Moments different

Not another “privacy toggle” buried in settings. Control sits in the publish flow:

| Pillar | What you get |
|--------|----------------|
| **Per-post audiences** | Six levels: everyone, mutuals, best friends, custom people, named lists, or only me |
| **Memory with context** | Map pins, archive, saved moments, and **Echoes** (friends’ perspectives of the same place within ~500 m and 24 hours) |
| **Active privacy** | **Blackout** (restricted posts appear black in screenshots), **incognito** (30 min/day without the usual trail), encrypted chat |
| **Less like-pressure** | Sixteen expressive reactions instead of a public like counter as the main signal |
| **Creation with play** | Stories with Reveal / Polaroid / Audio stickers, **Hidden Layers**, Story Chains, reels |
| **Chat in the same app** | Encrypted DMs, view-once, vanish timers, live location, buzz, rich shares of moments/stories/profiles |

Honest take: Instagram still wins on mass reach and creators. Moments wins when you want the right circle, context, and privacy that you actually use every day—not when you need Explore, ads, or shopping at scale.

## Hidden Layers

Some posts hide something more.

On a single-photo moment you can add up to three layers of text, audio, or images inside the photo. Friends discover them by tapping; you can schedule layers to unlock later.

A secret note. A voice. A clue. An image inside another. Something you don’t see at first—but it was there.

## Stories with more magic

Create stories with stickers to express yourself and tell what’s happening better:

- **Reveal** — scratch to uncover hidden content  
- **Polaroid** — an image with style and a caption  
- **Audio** — voice or ambient sound in the story  
- **Story Chains** — collaborative threads (up to 10 parts over 48 hours) for trips and nights out  

## Privacy without the hassle

You decide who sees each moment: best friends, custom audiences, only me, private lists, comment control, hide followers/following, hide content from specific people, block or mute accounts and words.

## Chat and real connections

Private encrypted messages with photos, videos, audio, ephemeral content, and view-once media. Share when you want, with who you want—including moments, stories, reels, and profiles as rich cards in the conversation.

## Nova

Nova is your in-app AI assistant—preferences, context, and useful suggestions without taking control away. Conversations with Nova are designed to feel personal and protected; publishing actions need your approval.
