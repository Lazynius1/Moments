moments v2.3 - Introducing "Echoes"

Get ready to relive your moments from every angle! In version 2.3, we introduce a brand new feature: Echoes. It's not just an update; it's a completely new way to connect through shared experiences.

What is an Echo? Have you ever been to a concert, party, or trip with friends where everyone captured the same moment? An Echo is a living story that unites all those perspectives into a single immersive experience.

What's New:

- 2D Perspectives: Swipe horizontally to jump between friends and see what they recorded. Swipe vertically to deep dive into each person's story.
- Full Privacy Control: Echoes maintain the same privacy and audience options you already know. They work for mutual connections. Once created, an Echo remains visible even if you stop being friends, unless the viewing privacy (either yours or other participants') of the original content changes. You decide who sees your content, with the same security as always.
- Social Privacy: Content unlocks automatically when 2 or more people accept the invitation, creating a trust pact between friends.
- Sign in with Apple: Fast and secure login with your Apple ID. Already have an account? You can now link it in Settings!
- Registration and Login: We have redesigned the interface for a smoother and more premium experience.

- Total Immersion: The Echo viewer is now 100% full screen, distraction-free.
- Smart Suggestions: Get notified when it's detected that you and your friends have coincided in the same place so you don't miss the chance to create an Echo.
- A final tip: "The best memories don't have a single owner. Swipe sideways in an Echo to see what your eyes didn't catch on their own."
- Bug Fixes: Interface improvements, Bug fixes and performance improvements

Requires location tags in moments, with a maximum distance of 500 meters and within a 24-hour window.

=== APPLE REVIEW NOTES ===

General:
This version introduces the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage user consent for personalized ads in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK.

Consent Flow:
1. Custom Intro Sheet: A custom explanatory screen is presented to inform the user about the upcoming privacy choices.
2. UMP Consent Form: If required (EEA/UK), the Google UMP SDK consent form is presented to collect GDPR consent (respecting IAB TCF v2.2).
3. ATT Authorization: Immediately following the UMP interaction, the app requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization through the standard system alert.
4. Ad Initialization: Consent and tracking status are then used to initialize AdMob with the appropriate personalization settings.

This sequence ensures full compliance with both GDPR standards and Apple's ATT guidelines.
